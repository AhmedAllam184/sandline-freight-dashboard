About Sandline

An interactive portfolio concept by Ahmed Allam. Sandline Freight, people, customers, vehicle records and operational results are fictional. Vehicle photography is AI-generated.

Map boundaries use Natural Earth public-domain geography. City coordinates use GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Routes show illustrative connections and are not surveyed roads, recommended routes or live GPS.

Anonymous page views and section visits are logged for demo usage analysis. No names, email addresses, raw IP addresses, form contents or precise visitor locations are recorded by the application. The tracker respects Do Not Track.
